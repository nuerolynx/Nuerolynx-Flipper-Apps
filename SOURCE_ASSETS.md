# Source asset policy

Nuerolynx Flipper Apps is a build-focused snapshot of Momentum Apps. It keeps application code, manifests, icons, runtime data, licenses, and text documentation.

The snapshot omits large files that are not consumed by the Flipper firmware build.

- Reference manuals in PDF and CHM formats
- Demo videos and animated screenshots
- Screenshots, photos, and marketplace banners
- Layered XCF and PSD design files

Those materials remain available from [Momentum Apps](https://github.com/Next-Flip/Momentum-Apps) and their original application projects. Removing them keeps the companion repository practical to clone without changing the compiled firmware or installed applications.

Original copyright notices and application licenses remain in their source directories.
